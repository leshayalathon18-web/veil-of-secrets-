# Veil of Secrets — Product Brief

## Vision

Veil of Secrets is an elegant social deduction mystery where the environment
remembers more accurately than the witnesses. Each match creates a new victim,
culprit, motive, timeline, evidence chain, witness account, room state, and
sequence of events. The challenge is not merely naming a suspect; it is proving
a complete theory.

The visual language is gothic luxury rather than horror: black marble,
oxblood velvet, antique gold, candlelight, rain, fog, carved libraries, grand
stairs, and restrained cinematic motion.

## Core match loop

1. **Arrival** — Players enter a match, select a detective identity, confirm
   voice settings, and vote on map and difficulty.
2. **Investigation** — Players search room-specific interaction points while
   the manor changes state around them.
3. **Synthesis** — Evidence, witness statements, and timeline fragments enter
   each player's notebook. Manual notes remain private.
4. **Discussion** — The group shares, withholds, or bluffs about findings.
   Nobody is eliminated from play.
5. **Accusation** — Each player submits culprit, method, location, motive,
   timeline, and supporting evidence.
6. **Reconstruction** — The truth is revealed as a timeline, scores are
   awarded, and progression is granted.

## Blackthorn Manor

The complete map includes the Grand Hall, Library, Kitchen, Dining Hall, Wine
Cellar, Master Bedroom, Guest Rooms, Study, Secret Passage, Garden, Ballroom,
Attic, Basement, and Observatory. Every room contains multiple search families
and state variants so evidence placement does not become rote.

## Multiplayer principles

- Server-authoritative match phases and mystery truth
- Reconnect support and no permanent mid-match elimination
- Private rooms, invite codes, friends, and public matchmaking
- Voice controls for push-to-talk, open mic, mute, per-player volume, voice
  activity, block, report, noise suppression, and echo cancellation
- Voice UI must remain outside critical evidence and accusation controls

## Progression

Progression is cosmetic only: detective outfits, badges, animations,
nameplates, notebook skins, lobby themes, voice effects, pets, and titles. No
mechanic may sell competitive power or privileged evidence.

## Accessibility and platforms

Desktop, tablet, and phone are equal product surfaces. Required support
includes colorblind-safe states, subtitles, large text, adjustable UI scale,
reduced motion, push-to-talk toggle, controller navigation, and keyboard
remapping.

## Performance targets

- 60+ FPS on mid-range hardware
- Fast first load and compact runtime memory
- Low-latency state replication and bounded network payloads
- Responsive input on touch, controller, and keyboard
