# Veil of Secrets — Development Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — First-launch vertical slice

Status: complete.

- Original Blackthorn visual system
- Opening, board-game rules, nine-room manor board, notebook, accusation, verdict
- Four detective pawns, animated dice, connected movement, rival turns
- Round pressure, room searches, and flipping manor-event cards
- Responsive layouts and accessibility preferences
- Production build, automated checks, share card, and deployment

## Milestone 2 — Multiplayer match shell

- Server-authoritative lobby and phase state machine
- Private invite codes and ready checks
- Character, map, and difficulty voting
- Reconnect and late-join policy
- Responsive player roster and voice-safe HUD regions
- Deterministic seed contract shared by server and client

## Milestone 3 — Procedural mystery engine

- Culprit, victim, motive, method, and location constraints
- Timeline dependency graph and contradiction validation
- Evidence placement rules and room-state variants
- Witness knowledge, truthfulness, and alibi generation
- Solvability scoring and automated impossible-case rejection

## Milestone 4 — Full investigation

- Blackthorn room navigation
- Search families for drawers, cabinets, desks, fireplaces, bookshelves,
  safes, windows, and furniture
- Evidence discovery, inspection, sharing, and chain-of-custody UI
- Private/manual notebook notes
- Ambient events, rain, lighting, particles, and authored soundscape

## Milestone 5 — Discussion, voice, and safety

- Discussion phase staging and timers
- Push-to-talk and open microphone
- Voice activity, mute, volume, noise suppression, and echo cancellation
- Block, report, moderation records, and safety feedback
- Accessibility captions where the platform permits them

## Milestone 6 — Progression and launch readiness

- Cosmetic inventory and rewards
- Statistics, titles, badges, and case history
- Friends and public matchmaking
- Controller and keyboard remapping
- Performance budgets, soak tests, network simulation, and security review
- Content pipeline for new maps, cases, suspects, and cosmetics
